AQZ Tech Solution is a SaaS and software marketing landing page built to promote a productivity-focused app with clear onboarding, pricing, and conversion paths.

## Overview

The site follows a modern app-launch template structure: hero value proposition, feature walkthrough, social proof, pricing plans, and app-store download calls to action.

## Core Capabilities

- Hero section positioning easy productivity for modern businesses
- Three-step onboarding flow: Download App, Create account, Install and enjoy
- Feature highlights: easy customize, fully responsive, free updates, ultimate support
- Tiered pricing: Basic ($0), Standard ($39), and Business ($49) per user per month
- Testimonials, FAQ, and blog/articles sections for trust and content marketing
- App Store and Google Play download CTAs

## Notable On-Page Signals

- 3.5k+ active customers and engagement metrics in hero and stats sections
- 14-day free trial messaging for new sign-ups
- Multi-section layout optimized for SaaS conversion funnels

## Intended Users

- SaaS founders launching a product marketing site
- Teams needing a fast React-based landing page template
- Businesses promoting mobile or web app downloads with pricing transparency
