Hive Portal is a US-focused SEO intelligence dashboard branded as an Intelligence Node for monitoring search climate, crawl health, and performance signals.

## Overview

The portal surfaces real-time and near-real-time SEO telemetry in a single authenticated workspace, combining GSC-live data, crawl stream monitoring, and Core Web Vitals in one view.

## Core Capabilities

- US SEO Climate with search volatility scoring
- GSC-live time windows: Now, 4h, 8h, 12h, and 24h
- Googlebot stream with per-URL crawl status (200, 301, 403, 404, indexed, crawled, discovered)
- Core Web Vitals panel with LCP, INP, and CLS plus mobile/desktop traffic split
- Traffic origins by US city and SERP feature visibility (Featured Snippets, People Also Ask, Local Pack)
- Secure portal authentication with work email and password

## Notable On-Page Signals

- Total Keywords (US) tracking with week-over-week change
- Core Web Vitals passing status with sub-second LCP and low CLS examples
- 72% visibility metric and live traffic origin percentages by metro

## Intended Users

- SEO teams monitoring US search performance across client properties
- Agencies managing crawl health and indexation for multiple domains
- Growth teams tracking volatility, CWV, and SERP feature exposure in one dashboard
